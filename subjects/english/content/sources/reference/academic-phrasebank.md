# Academic Phrasebank 功能分类

来源：University of Manchester, John Morley,
<https://www.phrasebank.manchester.ac.uk/>
（2026-09-14 查阅。页面上的具体短语是曼彻斯特大学的知识产权，这里只保存
分类，写作时打开原页挑骨架，不把整站短语库搬进仓库。）

## 论文章节

- Introducing work
- Referring to sources
- Describing methods
- Reporting results
- Discussing findings
- Writing conclusions

## 通用语言功能

- Being cautious
- Being critical
- Classifying and listing
- Compare and contrast
- Defining terms
- Describing trends
- Describing quantities
- Explaining causality
- Giving examples
- Signalling transition
- Writing about the past

英语二大作文用得上的主要是：compare and contrast、describing trends、
describing quantities、explaining causality、being cautious、signalling
transition。小作文用 introducing / concluding 的应用文骨架，不套研究论文口吻。
